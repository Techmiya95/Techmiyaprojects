Abstract: Forgery in documents is a major problem in the financial and legal sectors. This project implements a Signature Verification System using image processing and machine learning to compare a queried signature against a reference to determine its authenticity.

Introduction

Signatures are still a primary method of identity verification. The Signature Verification System uses computer vision to analyze structural features, pressure points, and stroke patterns in a signature image. It helps in detecting forgeries that are often difficult for the naked eye to identify.

Professional Title

Authenticity Verification of Handwritten Signatures using Siamese Networks

Advantages

  • Security Enhancement: Prevents fraudulent activities in banks and legal institutions.
  • Automated Processing: Speeds up the verification of large volumes of documents.
  • Objective Analysis: Removes human bias from the verification process.
  • Customizable: Can be trained for specific individuals for high-security applications.

Disadvantages

  • Natural Variation: Genuine signatures can change slightly over time or depending on the person's state.
  • Limited to Static Analysis: In this version, the system analyzes the image rather than the dynamic motion of signing.
  • Dataset Dependency: Requires multiple samples per person for high accuracy.

Conclusion

The Signature Verification System offers a robust technological solution to document safety. By integrating AI-based analysis, organizations can significantly reduce the risk of forgery. Ongoing research focuses on incorporating dynamic signature features captured through digital tablets.
