Skin Disease Detection System

Powered by Techmiya Projects

Abstract: Early detection of skin diseases is crucial for successful treatment. This project utilizes Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) to analyze skin images and classify various conditions like melanoma, psoriasis, and dermatitis. The system provides users with a quick diagnostic suggestion based on image processing.

Introduction

Skin disorders are widespread, but many individuals delay seeking medical help due to accessibility or cost issues. The Skin Disease Detection System aims to provide an accessible tool for early screening. Users can upload images of affected skin areas, which the system then processes through a deep learning model to identify potential diseases.

Professional Title

Dermatological Classification using Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN)

Advantages

  • Early Screening: Helps in identifying potential issues before they become severe.
  • User-Friendly: Simple interface for uploading images and getting results.
  • Supports Doctors: Acts as a secondary tool to assist dermatologists in diagnosis.
  • Data-Driven: Improves accuracy as more diverse image data is used for training.

Disadvantages

  • Not a Final Diagnosis: Results are based on visual analysis only and must be verified by a specialist.
  • Image Quality Issues: Poor quality or blurry images can lead to incorrect classifications.
  • Bias Risk: Model accuracy may vary across different skin tones if training data is not diverse.

Conclusion

The Skin Disease Detection System showcases the potential of AI in medical diagnostics. While it is a screening tool, its ability to provide instant feedback can save lives through early intervention. Future developments could include a mobile app for better accessibility in rural areas.
